# Surface brief: La Segundita marketing site (Home, Our Story, EN + ES)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: local shoppers and social followers, equally. Primary action: come visit the store (address, hours, directions). Secondary: follow on social, meet the family. Constraints: preserve all approved copy and every placeholder; English default with a whole-site Spanish switch (/es/); static export; custom CSS; no invented business facts or photos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a hand-painted shop rótulo and a rack of thrift tags set as a vintage Mexican magazine: each section is a printed chapter on its own colored stock. It refuses the cream-page-with-cards template and the split startup hero.

OWN-WORLD: Marigold, chocolate, dusty-rose, olive and paper stocks owning whole sections; burnt-orange/terracotta ink; Libre Caslon Display headlines with Caslon italic accents, DM Sans text; double print rules, punched thrift tags on string, photo corners, rubber stamps, butterflies and marigolds as printer's ornaments; drawn SVG icons only.

STORY: A neighbor or follower sees a warm family shop, believes the finds and the family are real, and leaves knowing where and when to come.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead with sign-lettered wordmark, nav and EN/ES tag. Marigold field; headline at display scale across the left; storefront print tilted right, overlapping the band below, stamp on its corner, See Inside toggle on its frame. Primary "Come Say Hola" filled terracotta; "Explore the Finds" as text link. A visit strip (address, hours, directions) closes the band.

FORM: Brief-pinned (Bright Mexican-vintage editorial); no concept-seed roll; code-led (no image generation available). Signature interaction: thrift tags that swing on hover/focus; storefront/interior shutter swap.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
